Know your cardiometabolic risk in 3 minutes

MedRisk estimates your 10-year risk of cardiovascular disease and type 2 diabetes from a short health questionnaire — and explains which factors drive your result in plain language. No lab visit required.

Start the assessment How it works

Guest mode — you do not need an account to try the prototype.

Prototype mockup (pre-defense 0, DIP-8). These pages are a design sketch, not the final design, and no machine-learning model is running here. Every number you see is an illustrative example, not a real model output. Layout, colours and wording will change after the user interviews and the supervisor's review.

1 · Answer

12 simple questions about age, blood pressure, cholesterol, habits and family history. Four measurement questions are optional — skip what you do not know.

2 · Estimate

Machine-learning models validated against clinical baselines (Framingham, SCORE-style) compute two risks for the same input.

3 · Understand

Your personal top-5 risk factors, ranked and explained — so you know what to discuss with your doctor first.

History (account)Past assessments and risk trends over time

MedRisk is an educational decision-support tool. It provides risk estimates, not diagnoses, and it does not recommend treatment. Always consult a licensed physician.

Who this prototype is for

  • Adults 30–70 — the primary user: no medical background, may have low health literacy.
  • Primary-care professionals — secondary audience (planned for a later milestone).
  • Administrators / researchers — manage models and evaluate model quality.

Know your cardiometabolic risk in 3 minutes

MedRisk estimates your 10-year risk of cardiovascular disease and type 2 diabetes from a short health questionnaire — and explains which factors drive your result in plain language. No lab visit required.

Start the assessment → How it works
Prototype mockup (pre-defense 0). These pages are an early design sketch, not the final design — layout, colors and texts will be revised after user feedback. Numbers below are illustrative; no real prediction model is running here, and the backend shown in the docs is an approximate variant that may change.

1 · Answer

12 simple questions about age, blood pressure, cholesterol, habits and family history. Guest mode — no account required.

2 · Estimate

Machine-learning models validated against clinical baselines (Framingham, SCORE-style) compute two risks for the same input.

3 · Understand

Your personal top-5 risk factors, ranked and explained — so you know what to discuss with your doctor first.

What you get

Report sectionContents
Cardiovascular risk10-year probability, category (low / moderate / high / very high)
Diabetes risk10-year probability, category, key metabolic factors
Factor attributionTop contributing factors with direction and relative magnitude (SHAP)
Next stepsPlain-language suggestions; what to measure next; doctor-visit guidance
History (account)Past assessments and risk trends over time

MedRisk is an educational decision-support tool. It provides risk estimates, not diagnoses. Always consult a licensed physician.

Who can help you interpret this result

  • Start with a primary-care physician (therapist / GP / family doctor). They can measure the values you left empty, check your blood pressure and interpret the numbers against your own history.
  • If you already have a diagnosis (for example hypertension or diabetes), your cardiologist or endocrinologist can use this report as one more input during your next visit.
  • Bring this page with you — the “What you entered” table and the factor list make a good starting point for the conversation.

Questions you can ask

  • Which of the measurements I left empty should I get, and where?
  • What do my blood pressure and cholesterol numbers mean for me?
  • How should I monitor my risk over the next year?

What this tool cannot tell you

  • Whether you have a disease — MedRisk estimates a probability for a group, not a diagnosis for an individual.
  • Which treatment, medicine or therapy you should take — that decision belongs to a qualified physician. Do not start or stop any medication because of this page.
  • Whether an urgent condition is developing. If you have chest pain, severe shortness of breath or fainting, seek emergency care immediately.
